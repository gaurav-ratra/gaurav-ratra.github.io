Tarot artwork used by the app: the 22 major arcana, 1024 x 1536 JPEG.
The app embeds 480 x 720 WebP copies (quality 68) of these files.
Source: tarot_cards_individual_artworks_jpg.zip (104 images, including alternates and 56 minor arcana cards that are not used yet).
In the app, card index 0 to 21 follows tarot-card-map.csv.
Four cards come from the gold-border style because the purple-neon versions have the wrong Roman numeral (or, for Death, do not exist in the zip).

card-back.png: the supplied card back (1024 x 1536). The app embeds a 480 x 720 WebP copy and uses it on every face-down card.

hands-deck.png: hands holding the deck (transparent PNG, 1199 x 1312), used as the Tarot home art. The app embeds a cropped 620 px WebP copy with alpha.
